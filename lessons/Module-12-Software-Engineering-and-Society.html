O‘quv maqsadlari

  • texnologiyaning ijtimoiy ta’sirini aniq tasvirlash;
  • bevosita va bilvosita oqibatlarni farqlash;
  • turli manfaatdor tomonlar nuqtayi nazarini hisobga olish;
  • foyda, xavf va yechimni muvozanatli maqolada birlashtirish.

Warm-up

  1. Software jamiyatdagi qaysi muammoni yaxshi hal qilgan?
  2. Raqamli xizmatdan kimlar foydalana olmay qolishi mumkin?
  3. Avtomatlashtirish qanday yangi ish yaratadi va qaysi ishni kamaytiradi?
  4. Dasturiy mahsulotning ekologik ta’siri bormi?

Technical vocabulary

Termin Sodda ta’rif Misol

Reading

A city moves public services to a mobile platform. Many residents save time, but older users and people without reliable internet struggle to apply for support. The city first treats this as a training problem, then discovers broader issues: inaccessible forms, expensive mobile data and the removal of face-to-face assistance. It keeps the digital service but adds accessible design, community training, public access points and an offline support channel. Innovation becomes more inclusive when its social context is considered.

Comprehension

  1. Who benefits from the mobile platform?
  2. Which groups face barriers?
  3. Why is training alone insufficient?
  4. Which four inclusive measures are introduced?

Language focus — balanced argument

  • On the one hand ... on the other hand ...
  • A major benefit is ...
  • A possible unintended consequence is ...
  • This affects ... because ...
  • A balanced solution would ...

Darajali topshiriqlar

Beginner — B1

100–120 so‘zda bitta texnologiyaning ikki foydasi, bitta xavfi va bitta yechimini yozing.

Intermediate — B1+

140–180 so‘zda raqamli xizmatni ikki stakeholder nuqtayi nazaridan tahlil qiling.

Advanced — B2

220–280 so‘zli yakuniy maqola yozing: context, benefits, risks, affected groups, evidence, solution va conclusion.

How should software engineers balance innovation and public responsibility?

Peer-review

  • Xulosa tezisga qaytadimi?

Self-assessment

  • Men digital divide va inclusion’ni tushuntirdim.

  • Men dalillangan yakuniy maqola yozdim.
  • Men kurs davomida o‘rgangan terminlardan foydalandim.
