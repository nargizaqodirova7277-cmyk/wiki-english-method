09-modul

Project Management Tools

Kasbiy ingliz tili, texnik fikrlash va hamkorlikdagi yozuv bir darsda.

Dars pasporti

Yo‘nalish
Software Engineering
Format
O‘rganish · Mashq · Yozish
Daraja
A2–B2

Vazifa, muddat va jamoaviy jarayonni boshqarish

Daraja Vaqt Yakuniy natija
A2–B290–110 daqiqaSprint rejasi va yozma status update

O‘quv maqsadlari

  • loyiha boshqaruvi terminlarini kontekstda ishlatish;
  • vazifa holati, ustuvorligi va muddatini bildirish;
  • to‘siq va bog‘liqlikni aniq tushuntirish;
  • qisqa loyiha yangilanishini yozish.

Warm-up

  1. Jamoa vazifalarni qayerda saqlashi kerak?
  2. Barcha vazifalar bir xil ustuvorlikdami?
  3. Muddat kechikishi oldindan qanday aniqlanadi?
  4. Yaxshi status update nimalarni o‘z ichiga oladi?

Technical vocabulary

Termin Sodda ta’rif Misol
milestonean important project pointThe prototype is our first milestone.
backloga list of planned workThe team prioritized the backlog.
sprinta short fixed work periodThe sprint lasts two weeks.
assigneethe person responsible for a taskEach task has one assignee.
prioritythe importance of workSecurity fixes have high priority.
dependencywork relying on other workTesting has a dependency on deployment.
blockera problem stopping progressMissing access is the current blocker.
status updatea short progress reportThe leader shared a weekly status update.

Reading

A student team tracks work in several chat messages. Tasks are forgotten, two people implement the same feature and testing starts late. The team creates one shared board with a backlog, priority, assignee and deadline for every task. During a short daily meeting, members report progress and blockers. At the end of the sprint, they review completed work and move unfinished items back to the backlog. Visibility improves coordination without adding long meetings.

Comprehension

  1. Which problems occur when tasks stay in chat?
  2. What information is added to every task?
  3. What do members report daily?
  4. What happens to unfinished work?

Language focus — project status

  • The task is planned / in progress / blocked / completed.
  • We have completed ...
  • We are currently working on ...
  • The main blocker is ...
  • We expect to finish by ...

Darajali topshiriqlar

Beginner — A2

Beshta vazifadan iborat task board yozing: task, assignee, status va deadline.

Intermediate — B1

100–140 so‘zli weekly status update yozing: completed, current, blocker va next step.

Advanced — B2

180–220 so‘zda ikki haftalik sprint rejasini asoslang: goal, backlog priority, dependencies, risks va success criteria.

Wiki writing

Plan a small software project for a four-person team.

Kamida 6 ta termin, aniq mas’ullar va real muddatlardan foydalaning.

Peer-review

  • Har vazifaning egasi aniqmi?
  • Ustuvorlik asoslanganmi?
  • Dependency va blocker ko‘rsatilganmi?
  • Reja belgilangan muddatga mosmi?

Self-assessment

  • Men task holatini aniq bildira olaman.
  • Men blocker va dependency farqini bilaman.
  • Men qisqa status update yozdim.
  • Men rejamga o‘lchanadigan natija qo‘shdim.
