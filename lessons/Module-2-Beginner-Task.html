A1–A2: Choose the best action

Har bir vaziyat uchun eng yaxshi harakatni tanlang va sababini bitta gap bilan yozing.

  1. Two developers are working on the same file.
  • A) They do not communicate.
  • B) They use version control and discuss changes.
  1. A team member does not understand the task.
  • A) They ask for clarification.
  • B) They ignore the task.
  1. The deadline is close.
  • A) The team checks priorities.
  • B) The team starts unrelated work.

Writing

Quyidagi qoliplar yordamida 50–80 so‘z yozing:

  • A good IT team needs ...
  • Each member should ...
  • Clear communication helps ...
  • The team leader ...
