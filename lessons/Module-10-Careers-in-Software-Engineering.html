  • Software Engineering rollarini aniq tavsiflash;

Technical vocabulary

Termin Sodda ta’rif Misol
rolea defined job in a teamA QA engineer has a quality-focused role.
qualificationknowledge or experience requiredThe position requires a technical qualification.
hard skillmeasurable technical abilitySQL is a useful hard skill.
soft skillability to work effectively with peopleCommunication is an essential soft skill.
portfolioevidence of completed workHer portfolio includes three web projects.
internshipsupervised entry-level work experienceThe internship provides practical experience.
job descriptiona document explaining a positionRead the job description before applying.
career patha sequence of professional developmentHis career path leads toward system architecture.

Reading

Dilnoza wants to become a backend developer. She reads several junior job descriptions and finds repeated requirements: one programming language, databases, version control, testing and communication. Instead of trying to learn everything at once, she creates a six-month plan. Each month has one measurable goal and a small portfolio project. She also joins code reviews and applies for an internship. Her plan combines technical study, visible evidence and teamwork experience.

Comprehension

  1. Which requirements repeat in job descriptions?
  2. Why does Dilnoza avoid learning everything at once?
  3. How does she provide evidence of her skills?
  4. Which activity develops teamwork experience?

Language focus — goals and evidence

  • I aim to become ...
  • I have experience with ...
  • My portfolio demonstrates ...
  • By December, I will have completed ...
  • This skill is relevant because ...

Darajali topshiriqlar

Beginner — A2

60–80 so‘zda tanlagan kasbingiz, uchta zarur skill va bitta kelgusi qadamingizni yozing.

Intermediate — B1

Vakansiyadagi beshta talabni o‘z hozirgi ko‘nikmalaringiz bilan 100–140 so‘zda taqqoslang.

Advanced — B2

180–220 so‘zli olti oylik career development plan yozing: goal, milestones, evidence, resources va review date.

Peer-review

Self-assessment

  • Men kasbiy rolni aniq tavsiflay olaman.

  • Men o‘lchanadigan muddatli maqsad yozdim.
  • Men natijani isbotlaydigan dalil belgiladim.
