Ethics for Developers

Kasbiy ingliz tili, texnik fikrlash va hamkorlikdagi yozuv bir darsda.

Dars pasporti

Yo‘nalish
Software Engineering
Format
O‘rganish · Mashq · Yozish
Daraja
A2–B2

Dasturchining kasbiy mas’uliyati

Daraja Vaqt Yakuniy natija
B1–B290–110 daqiqaEtik vaziyat bo‘yicha dalillangan qaror

O‘quv maqsadlari

  • asosiy etik tushunchalarni kontekstda ishlatish;
  • manfaatdor tomonlarni va mumkin bo‘lgan zararni aniqlash;
  • qaror variantlarini tamoyillar asosida taqqoslash;
  • mas’uliyatli tavsiyani dalillar bilan himoya qilish.

Warm-up

  1. Qonuniy qaror har doim etik qarormi?
  2. Foydalanuvchi roziligi qanday olinishi kerak?
  3. Dasturchi zararli talabni rad eta oladimi?
  4. Algoritmdagi adolatsizlik uchun kim javobgar?

Technical vocabulary

Termin Sodda ta’rif Misol
privacycontrol over personal informationPrivacy requires limited data collection.
consentinformed agreementUsers gave consent before sharing location.
transparencyopenness about decisions and processesTransparency explains how recommendations work.
accountabilityresponsibility for actions and resultsThe company accepted accountability for the failure.
fairnessequal and unbiased treatmentThe team tested the system for fairness.
conflict of interestcompeting personal and professional interestsThe reviewer declared a conflict of interest.
intellectual propertylegally protected creative workThe license protects intellectual property.
stakeholdera person affected by a decisionStudents are key stakeholders in the project.

Reading

A company asks a developer to add hidden location tracking to a free application. The data may improve advertising, but users are not clearly informed. The developer identifies affected stakeholders and explains that hidden tracking violates meaningful consent. She proposes an optional feature with a clear explanation, limited collection and a delete-data control. The revised approach may collect less data, but it respects privacy and reduces legal and reputational risk.

Comprehension

  1. Why does the company want location data?
  2. Which ethical principle is missing?
  3. What alternative does the developer propose?
  4. Which risks does the alternative reduce?

Language focus — ethical reasoning

  • The team has a duty to ...
  • This decision may harm ...
  • Users have the right to ...
  • A more responsible option is ...
  • The benefit does / does not justify the risk because ...

Darajali topshiriqlar

Beginner — B1

80–100 so‘zda privacy muammosini, affected stakeholder’ni va bitta yechimni yozing.

Intermediate — B1+

120–160 so‘zda ikki qaror variantini fairness, consent va harm bo‘yicha taqqoslang.

Advanced — B2

180–220 so‘zli ethics memo yozing: facts, stakeholders, principles, options, consequences va recommendation.

Wiki writing

Peer-review

  • Fakt va taxmin ajratilganmi?
  • Barcha muhim stakeholders ko‘rsatilganmi?
  • Foyda va zarar muvozanatli baholanganmi?
  • Tavsiya etik tamoyil bilan asoslanganmi?

Self-assessment

  • Men stakeholder va mumkin bo‘lgan zararni aniqladim.
  • Men consent, privacy va fairness’ni farqlay olaman.
  • Men kamida ikki variantni taqqosladim.
  • Men tavsiyam uchun aniq sabab berdim.
